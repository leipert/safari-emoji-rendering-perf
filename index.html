Repainting text with many emoji is slow

Source, results, and a profile: github.com/leipert/safari-emoji-rendering-perf

The page draws a 100×100 grid of ⬛️ and ⬜️ emoji (10,000 emoji, like a large QR code drawn with emoji) and changes the text of its middle line on every animation frame for 4 seconds, as typing in that line does. A browser that keeps up renders a frame every 16.7 ms, about 240 frames.

Variants: emoji · ASCII instead of emoji · emoji, each line in its own block · emoji, type in the middle line yourself

Add size=29 (a typical QR code), 50, or 75 to change the grid size, and combine parameters, for example ?type=1&lines=block.
